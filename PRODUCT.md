# Product

## Platform

web

## Stack

Vite, React, JSX, and Tailwind CSS, as specified by the user.

## Users

People who need a damaged or unreliable mobile phone diagnosed and repaired, and who want a clear, reassuring path to book service.

## Product Purpose

Max Mobbiles presents mobile repair services and helps visitors understand the process, explore common repairs, and submit a repair request.

## Positioning

Premium presentation, clear service choices, and an interactive booking flow designed to make mobile repair feel transparent and dependable.

## Operating Context

Visitors may arrive on a damaged phone, compare repair options quickly, and need to start a booking with minimal typing.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The site must use React with JSX, Vite, and Tailwind CSS.
- The experience must be responsive, interactive, and optimized for mobile use.
- Contact details, business location, exact prices, warranty terms, and supported device inventory are open decisions.
- Confirmed store location: Christian Colony, Vanasthalipuram, Hyderabad, Telangana 500070, using the Google Maps place link supplied by the user.
- The booking experience is a front-end request flow until a real backend or contact endpoint is provided.

## Brand Commitments

- Brand name: Max Mobbiles, preserving the spelling supplied by the user.
- Premium and aesthetic presentation.
- Light colors only.
- Professional, prompt, and interactive experience.
- Separate Home, Repairs, Process, About, and Contact pages with richer promotional content, video, effects, and purposeful animation.

## Evidence on Hand

No verified testimonials, commercial metrics, pricing, address, phone number, warranty policy, or service-level claims were supplied. Future work must not fabricate them.

## Product Principles

- Make the next repair action obvious within seconds.
- Reduce anxiety through clear language and visible process.
- Keep interaction fast and usable on a damaged or small-screen phone.
- Present a premium experience without compromising clarity.
